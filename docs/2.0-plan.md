# Interceptors 2.0 plan

The user's wishlist, grouped by what it takes. Order to be confirmed with the user.

## Phase A: no downloads needed

| # | Feature | How |
|---|---|---|
| 10, 11 | Broadcast overlay + biome | Toggleable stream-style HUD layer (key B): wind, EF, distance, vehicle status, anchor telemetry, current biome, clean for OBS capture. |
| 4 | Wind roar | Looping roar sound whose volume and pitch follow the tornadic wind at the camera; ducks our engine/horn sounds near the core. Layered on top of PMWeather's own tornado audio, not replacing it. |
| 5 | Big hail | Uses PMWeather's `getHail`: clattering-on-the-roof sound inside vehicles, a visibility fog/overlay that thickens with hail, dents and cracked glass on the vehicle, hurts people outside. |
| 8 | Visible damage | Part conditions shown on the model: dent overlay on armor, cracked then shattered glass, outer panels torn off at low armor. |
| 2 | Satellite dish | Roof dish (Starlink-style) block/attachment per vehicle. No dish = no online apps on the vehicle's screens (radar feed, storm list); the Intercept arrow still works off line of sight. DOW's radar counts as a dish. |
| 3 | Laptop battery | MacBook / Rugged Laptop / iPad get a battery that drains while open; a vehicle charger (the passenger mount) recharges them from the vehicle's electrical system, which draws on the engine (and the van's solar). |

## Phase B: more vehicles and polish

| # | Feature | How |
|---|---|---|
| 7 | TIV 2 (original design), Dom 1/2/3 more authentic | TIV 2: wedge body + rotating armored camera turret, our own design (Tornado Intercept addon is All Rights Reserved, nothing copied). Dom 3: gull-wing doors, air suspension drop, probe launcher, winch, LEVEL keypad (from the research). |
| 6 | Texture revamp | Continue the procedural materials: thicker plates, weld seams, more mud and grime, per-vehicle decals. |

## Phase C: needs a download (ask first)

| # | Feature | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Passenger web browser | MCEF 2.x exists for NeoForge 1.21.1 (Modrinth). Optional dependency; our Web app becomes a real browser while seated. Heavy on a 16 GB Mac (~300-500 MB), so it stays optional with the built-in apps as fallback. |
| 12 | Simple Voice Chat | Optional compat via the SVC API (needs its API jar): muffle/distort voices of players near the core, radio crackle between vehicles. |

## Not in the mod

| # | Feature | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 9 | Auto-stream to OBS/Streamlabs | Would mean putting a stream key in the mod and shipping a streaming client. Run OBS yourself and capture the game; #10 gives a stream-friendly overlay, and SVC voices go to OBS like any game audio. |
